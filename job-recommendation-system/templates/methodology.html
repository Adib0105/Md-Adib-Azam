Seven factors, one compatibility score

The overall score is the weighted sum of the factors below. Each factor is scored from 0 to 100, and the final result is rounded to one decimal place. The number is a model compatibility score, never a probability of getting hired.

Confidence means available information

High, Medium and Low indicate how much profile context you have supplied. They are heuristic completeness labels, not calibrated statistical confidence. An empty field is not automatically treated as a poor qualification.

What the simulator changes

Selected skills are added to a temporary copy of your profile. Job features and weights stay fixed, and the complete ranking is recalculated. Your profile and stored history remain unchanged. Text normalization can sometimes reduce a score, and that result is shown honestly.

The data behind this demonstration

The included jobs, companies, salary ranges and company ratings are generated examples. Charts describe this dataset only. There is no live job feed or external employer application integration. The application tracker is a personal local record.

What this model does not know

It cannot verify proficiency, resume claims, degree accreditation, work authorization, company culture or hiring outcomes. The education hierarchy approximates degree level and does not certify subject equivalence. Resume extraction is dictionary and pattern based; always review it. Scanned PDFs require OCR outside this app.

Your data stays on this computer

The app uses a local SQLite database. No paid APIs, account integrations or cloud inference are needed. Uploaded file bytes are discarded after extraction. Reviewed resume text is stored locally and can be removed from your profile.

Source context and equal-score tie-breakers

The original seven-factor match remains a 0–100 weighted score. Equal scores are ordered by employment-type fit (0–1), explicit remote-preference fit (0–1), source freshness (0–2) and information availability (0–1), then job ID. Information confidence describes available profile fields, not statistical confidence.

Unknown external experience and education use a neutral 50. Salary uses a neutral 50 when currencies differ, pay periods are not annual or figures are missing; no currency conversion is assumed. Skills extracted from provider snippets can be incomplete. Admin weights affect new recommendations, while historical snapshots retain their original weights.

Listings last seen more than the configured stale window, older than the maximum age, explicitly marked possibly expired, or past a stated deadline are excluded from discovery. The default windows are 14 days since refresh and 60 days since posting. Catalog queries rank at most {{ config.MAX_RANKING_JOBS }} filtered, recent eligible jobs. Provider results are paginated upstream and then filtered locally.
