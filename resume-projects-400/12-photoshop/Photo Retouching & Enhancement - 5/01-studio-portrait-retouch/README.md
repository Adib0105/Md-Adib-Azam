# Studio Portrait Retouch

## Goal
Create a natural professional studio portrait retouch without changing the subject's identity.

## Required deliverables
- `Studio-Portrait-Retouch.psd` — layered editable source
- `preview.jpg` or `preview.png` — final result

## Photoshop skills
Camera Raw adjustments, healing, frequency separation or controlled skin cleanup, dodge & burn, color balance, sharpening and non-destructive layers.
