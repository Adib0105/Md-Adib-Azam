# Skin Retouch & Beauty Edit

## Goal
Produce a polished beauty retouch while preserving realistic skin texture.

## Required deliverables
- `Skin-Retouch-Beauty-Edit.psd`
- `preview.jpg` or `preview.png`

## Photoshop skills
Healing brush, clone stamp, masks, dodge & burn, selective color, luminosity control and texture-preserving retouching.
