# Monthly Social Report — Analysis Framework

The CSV contains simulated portfolio metrics.

## What to Review
- Reach and frequency by platform
- Engagement rate, not raw engagements alone
- Saves/shares as usefulness signals
- Link-click rate
- Lead quality and downstream conversion
- Best content pillar and format

## Example Actions
- Repurpose Instagram posts with high saves into carousels or guides.
- Evaluate LinkedIn on qualified clicks/leads rather than raw reach.
- Improve YouTube hooks and thumbnail/title alignment before increasing volume.
- Stop repeating content themes that get reach but no meaningful action.
