# Social Media Monthly Performance Report

## Objective
Build a monthly reporting system that explains what content worked, what did not and what should change next month.

## Metrics
Reach, impressions, engagement, saves, shares, profile actions, follower growth, link clicks and leads where available.

## Analysis Framework
Top posts → winning hooks/formats → audience response → weak content → next-month experiments.

## Reporting Rule
Avoid vanity-metric-only reporting. Connect content metrics to profile visits, enquiries, website actions or other business goals.

## Deliverables
Monthly scorecard, top-content analysis, lessons learned, next-month content recommendations and experiment backlog.
