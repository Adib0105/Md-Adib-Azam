# Digital Marketing Funnel Strategy

## Objective
Create a full-funnel marketing system that assigns the right message, channel and KPI to each stage of the customer journey.

## Funnel
Awareness → Consideration → Conversion → Retention → Advocacy.

## Channel Role
Content/social for discovery, SEO/search for intent capture, remarketing/email for follow-up and retention communication for repeat value.

## Measurement
Use stage-specific KPIs instead of judging every channel only by last-click conversions.

## Target KPIs
Reach, engaged visits, leads, conversion rate, CPA, repeat conversion and retention.

## Deliverables
Funnel map, channel-role matrix, message framework, KPI mapping and optimization cadence.
