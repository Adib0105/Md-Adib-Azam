# Customer Persona & Buyer Journey Project

## Objective
Develop evidence-oriented customer personas and map their information needs, objections and actions through the buying journey.

## Persona Fields
Role/life stage, goals, pain points, buying triggers, objections, preferred channels and decision criteria.

## Journey Stages
Problem awareness → research → comparison → decision → onboarding → retention.

## Marketing Use
Use the persona to guide content topics, ad angles, landing-page copy, email follow-up and sales qualification.

## Deliverables
Primary persona, secondary persona, journey map, objection matrix and message recommendations.

## Success Measures
Lead quality, content engagement, conversion rate and reduced mismatch between message and audience.
