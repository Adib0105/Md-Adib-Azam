# Measurement Notes

This 90-day plan is a portfolio strategy framework. Targets must be based on actual baseline data before a live campaign is judged.

## Measurement Rules
1. Define one primary business outcome per campaign.
2. Validate tracking before optimization.
3. Separate volume metrics from quality metrics.
4. Review lead quality with downstream sales/customer data where available.
5. Document every major budget, audience, creative or landing-page change.
6. Scale only after a repeatable signal appears — not from one strong day.
