# Go Digital Pro — Complete 90-Day Digital Marketing Plan

## Objective
Create an integrated 90-day growth plan for a digital marketing agency focused on authority, inbound leads and consistent outreach.

## Month 1 — Foundation
Positioning, service pages, analytics setup, content pillars, local SEO basics and lead-capture path.

## Month 2 — Growth
Consistent social publishing, SEO content, prospecting, lead magnet/free audit CTA and small paid tests where justified.

## Month 3 — Optimization
Double down on winning content, improve landing pages, retarget engaged users, strengthen email follow-up and review lead quality.

## Core Channels
Instagram, LinkedIn, SEO/content, email/outreach and selective paid media.

## Target KPIs
Qualified leads, consultation bookings, organic clicks, engagement quality, conversion rate and cost per qualified lead.

## Deliverables
90-day roadmap, channel plan, content cadence, funnel, KPI dashboard structure and weekly review process.
