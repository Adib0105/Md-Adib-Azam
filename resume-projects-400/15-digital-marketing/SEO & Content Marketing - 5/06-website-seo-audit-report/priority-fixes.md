# SEO Priority Fixes

Portfolio audit framework; no claim that a live site was crawled.

## P1 — Crawl & Indexing
- Confirm canonical domain and HTTPS redirects.
- Validate robots.txt and XML sitemap.
- Remove accidental noindex directives from important pages.

## P2 — Search Intent & On-page
- Give every commercial page one clear primary query.
- Rewrite titles/H1s to match intent without keyword stuffing.
- Strengthen thin pages with proof, FAQs and useful specifics.

## P3 — Technical UX
- Improve LCP/INP/CLS.
- Repair broken internal links and orphan pages.
- Compress oversized media.

## P4 — Measurement
Track impressions, clicks, indexed pages, conversions and landing-page engagement before/after changes.
