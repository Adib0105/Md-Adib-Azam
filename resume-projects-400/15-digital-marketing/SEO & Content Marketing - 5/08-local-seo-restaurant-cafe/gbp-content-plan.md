# Google Business Profile Content Plan

## Weekly Rhythm
- Monday: menu/product highlight
- Wednesday: behind-the-scenes photo
- Friday: weekend update or event
- Sunday: customer FAQ / accessibility / parking information

## Review Response Framework
Thank customer → reference specific feedback → invite them back. Do not incentivize positive reviews or fabricate testimonials.

## Local Landing Page Elements
Address, map, opening hours, menu/services, transport/parking, FAQs, photos and a clear booking/call CTA.
