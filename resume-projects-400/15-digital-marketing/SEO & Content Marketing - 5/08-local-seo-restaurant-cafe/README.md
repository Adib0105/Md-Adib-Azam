# Local SEO Strategy for Restaurant / Cafe

## Objective
Improve local search visibility for a restaurant or cafe and increase direction requests, calls and reservation actions.

## Strategy
- Optimize Google Business Profile categories, description and services
- Maintain consistent NAP information
- Publish local photos and updates
- Build review-request workflow
- Create location-focused website content
- Add relevant local internal links

## Local Content Ideas
Menu highlights, neighborhood guides, event pages, seasonal offers and nearby landmark content.

## Target KPIs
Local impressions, map views, calls, direction requests, website clicks, reviews and reservation conversions.

## Deliverables
Local SEO checklist, review strategy, content plan and monthly reporting framework.
