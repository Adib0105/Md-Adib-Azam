# 30-Day SEO Content Calendar

## Objective
Create a one-month search-led publishing plan that connects keyword intent to useful content and conversion pages.

## Content Structure
- 4 commercial/service articles
- 8 informational posts
- 4 comparison/problem-solution pieces
- 4 local-intent pages/posts
- Refresh and internal-link existing pages

## Workflow
Keyword cluster → search intent → content brief → draft → on-page optimization → internal links → publish → measure.

## Quality Checklist
Clear intent match, unique value, descriptive headings, useful examples, natural keyword use and strong CTA.

## Deliverables
30-day publishing schedule, topic clusters, content brief template and measurement plan.

## KPIs
Indexed pages, impressions, clicks, rankings, engagement and assisted conversions.
