# On-page Brief Template

For every new or refreshed page:

- **Primary intent:** informational / commercial / local
- **Primary topic:** one clear search need
- **Supporting questions:** 3–6 relevant subtopics
- **Title:** useful and specific; avoid stuffing
- **H1:** natural version of main topic
- **Proof:** examples, process, screenshots, data or experience where available
- **Internal links:** one parent page + 2–4 relevant supporting pages
- **CTA:** matched to intent
- **Measurement:** impressions, clicks, engagement and conversion action
