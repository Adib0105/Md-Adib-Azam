# Competitor SEO Analysis

## Objective
Compare three competing websites to identify keyword, content and page-structure opportunities for an SEO growth plan.

## Comparison Areas
- Ranking topic coverage
- Search intent served
- Landing-page structure
- Content depth
- Internal linking patterns
- Title/meta positioning
- Local and commercial pages

## Gap Analysis
Classify opportunities into quick wins, missing commercial pages, missing informational content and authority-building topics.

## Deliverables
Competitor comparison matrix, content-gap list, keyword opportunities and prioritized recommendations.

## KPIs
Share of ranking keywords, organic visibility, new page rankings and traffic to priority landing pages.
