# SEO Gap Analysis

This is a portfolio framework, not a claim about named real competitors.

## Gaps to Exploit
1. High-intent service pages with clearer proof and FAQs.
2. Topic clusters linking educational content to commercial pages.
3. Stronger local information where the business genuinely operates.
4. Faster, cleaner pages with simpler conversion paths.
5. Content refresh process for declining or outdated pages.

## Decision Rule
Prioritize gaps where **business value × search intent × feasibility** is highest, not simply the highest-volume keyword.
