# Keyword Research — 100 Keywords Project

## Objective
Build a 100-keyword research framework for a service business and group keywords by intent, funnel stage and content opportunity.

## Keyword Types
- Informational
- Commercial investigation
- Transactional
- Local intent
- Branded/non-branded

## Process
Seed topics → keyword expansion → intent classification → topic clustering → priority scoring → page/content mapping.

## Prioritization
Balance relevance, search demand, competition level, conversion intent and business value rather than chasing volume alone.

## Deliverables
100-keyword master list, intent labels, topic clusters, landing-page mapping and content opportunities.

## KPIs
Ranked keywords, organic clicks, CTR, landing-page conversions and non-branded traffic growth.
