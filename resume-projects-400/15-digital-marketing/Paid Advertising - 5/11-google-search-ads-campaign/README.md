# Google Search Ads Campaign

## Objective
Design a search campaign for a service business focused on high-intent leads rather than broad traffic.

## Campaign Structure
Separate ad groups by service theme and search intent. Use tightly related keywords, responsive search ads and dedicated landing-page messaging.

## Keyword Approach
Prioritize exact/phrase-match commercial intent, maintain negative-keyword lists and review search terms regularly.

## Ad Framework
Problem/Service + Benefit + Trust + Clear CTA.

## Target KPIs
CTR, conversion rate, cost per lead, search impression share and qualified lead rate.

## Deliverables
Campaign structure, keyword grouping, negative-keyword plan, ad-copy framework, landing-page alignment and optimization checklist.
