# E-commerce Conversion Tracking Plan

## Core Events
1. View content / product
2. Add to cart
3. Begin checkout
4. Purchase
5. Revenue/value

## Diagnostics
- Verify events fire once per action.
- Confirm currency and value parameters.
- Separate test orders from production reporting.
- Use UTM parameters for campaign/source/creative.
- Compare ad-platform attribution with analytics/store data rather than assuming either is perfect.

## Optimization Order
Fix tracking → validate landing page → evaluate creative/audience → adjust budget.
