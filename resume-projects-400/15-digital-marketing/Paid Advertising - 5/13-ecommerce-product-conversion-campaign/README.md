# E-commerce Product Conversion Campaign

## Objective
Plan a conversion-focused paid media campaign for an online product with clear prospecting, product-page and retargeting stages.

## Funnel
Discovery Ad → Product Page → Add to Cart → Checkout → Purchase → Retention.

## Creative Mix
Product demo, problem-solution, customer proof, feature comparison and offer-led creative.

## Optimization Areas
Creative fatigue, product-page clarity, price/offer communication, checkout friction and audience quality.

## Target KPIs
CTR, add-to-cart rate, checkout rate, conversion rate, CPA and ROAS.

## Deliverables
Funnel map, campaign structure, creative angle matrix, KPI plan and weekly optimization checklist.
