# Meta Lead Campaign Audience Plan

## Cold Audiences
- Local geography relevant to service delivery
- Broad demographic fit where appropriate
- Interest groups only when they genuinely improve relevance

## Warm Audiences
- 30-day profile engagers
- 30-day video viewers
- Website visitors where consent/tracking permits
- Lead-form opens without submission

## Exclusions
- Existing customers when acquisition-only
- Recent converted leads
- Employees/internal traffic where possible

## Testing
Run audience, creative and offer tests separately so the source of improvement is understandable.
