# Meta Facebook & Instagram Lead Campaign

## Objective
Create a Meta lead-generation campaign for a local service business using clear audience testing and lead qualification.

## Structure
Campaign → cold prospecting ad sets → creative variations → instant form / WhatsApp conversion path.

## Creative Angles
Pain point, benefit, social proof, offer and educational hook.

## Lead Form
Name, contact, requirement, budget range and preferred callback time.

## Optimization Plan
Test one variable at a time: audience, hook, visual or CTA. Pause weak creatives only after enough delivery for a fair comparison.

## Target KPIs
Thumb-stop rate, CTR, cost per lead, qualified lead rate and booking rate.
