# YouTube Ads Campaign Strategy

## Objective
Build a video advertising strategy that moves users from awareness to consideration and measurable website action.

## Creative Structure
0–5 sec hook → problem/context → solution → proof → CTA.

## Audience Layers
Custom intent/search themes, relevant interests, remarketing audiences and first-party visitors where available.

## Video Variants
15-second direct response, 30-second explainer and longer proof/education version.

## Target KPIs
View rate, cost per view, click-through rate, engaged visits, conversions and assisted conversions.

## Deliverables
Audience plan, script framework, campaign structure, CTA strategy and reporting dashboard outline.
