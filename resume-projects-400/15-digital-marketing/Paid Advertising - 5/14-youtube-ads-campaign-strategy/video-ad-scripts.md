# YouTube Ad Script Concepts

## 15-second — Problem / Solution
**0–3s:** “Running marketing without knowing what is working?”

**3–10s:** Show a simple dashboard and channel plan. “Connect content, ads and analytics around one measurable goal.”

**10–15s:** “Start with a clear 30-day plan.”

CTA: **View the strategy**

## 30-second — Educational
Hook: “Before spending more on ads, check these three things.”

1. Does the offer match the audience?
2. Does the landing page continue the same message?
3. Is the conversion event actually tracked?

Close: “Better measurement makes better optimization possible.”
