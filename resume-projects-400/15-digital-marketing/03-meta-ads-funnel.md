# 03. Meta Ads Funnel

> Resume-aligned Digital Marketing portfolio project by **Md Adib Azam**.

## Business goal

Build a practical meta ads funnel workflow that solves a real user or business problem and produces measurable, reviewable output.

## Deliverables

- Clear problem statement, target audience and success metric
- Research/input checklist and structured production workflow
- Final output specification with accessibility and quality checks
- Measurement plan covering reach, conversion, accuracy, time saved or risk reduced

## Implementation plan

1. Define the audience, constraints, inputs and expected output.
2. Create the first version using the tools named in this category.
3. Test with three representative cases and document observations.
4. Improve clarity, consistency, performance and error handling.
5. Export the final deliverable and write a short case-study summary.

## Acceptance criteria

- Reproducible workflow with no missing steps
- Original portfolio output, not a copied tutorial
- Mobile/readability check where applicable
- Honest metrics and documented limitations

## Resume talking point

“Designed and documented **Meta Ads Funnel**, translating requirements into a structured digital marketing deliverable with measurable quality checks.”
