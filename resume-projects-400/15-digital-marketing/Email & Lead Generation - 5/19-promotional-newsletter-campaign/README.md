# Promotional Newsletter Campaign

## Objective
Build a promotional newsletter that communicates one focused offer without sacrificing readability or subscriber trust.

## Structure
Headline → value proposition → featured offer → supporting proof/content → primary CTA → footer/preferences.

## Segmentation
Adapt messaging for engaged subscribers, previous buyers/leads and less-active contacts instead of sending identical copy to everyone.

## Testing
Subject line, hero message, CTA wording and send time.

## Target KPIs
Open rate, click-through rate, conversion rate, revenue/leads per send and unsubscribe rate.

## Deliverables
Newsletter wireframe, copy structure, segmentation plan and A/B testing framework.
