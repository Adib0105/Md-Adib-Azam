# E-commerce Abandoned Cart Email Campaign

## Objective
Recover high-intent shoppers who added products to cart but did not complete checkout.

## Sequence
- Email 1: reminder within a short delay
- Email 2: product benefit + objection handling
- Email 3: urgency or limited incentive where commercially appropriate

## Content Elements
Cart summary, clear return-to-cart CTA, trust signals, delivery/returns reassurance and support option.

## Target KPIs
Recovered carts, click rate, conversion rate, revenue per recipient and unsubscribe rate.

## Deliverables
Three-email sequence strategy, subject-line matrix, CTA copy and A/B testing plan.
