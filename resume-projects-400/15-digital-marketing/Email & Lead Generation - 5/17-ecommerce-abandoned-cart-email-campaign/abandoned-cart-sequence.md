# Abandoned Cart Email Sequence

## Email 1 — Reminder / 1 hour
**Subject:** You left something behind

Your selected item is still in your cart. Return when you’re ready.

**CTA:** Return to cart

## Email 2 — Friction Reducer / 20 hours
**Subject:** Questions before you order?

Highlight shipping, returns, sizing and support information. Do not invent scarcity.

**CTA:** Review order details

## Email 3 — Final Reminder / 48 hours
**Subject:** Still thinking it over?

Show the product, key benefits and genuine availability. Use an incentive only if it is a real business offer.

**CTA:** Complete checkout
