# B2B Cold Email Lead Generation Campaign

## Objective
Create a respectful, targeted outbound email framework for reaching relevant business prospects with a clear value proposition.

## Prospect Criteria
Industry, company size, role relevance, likely problem and service fit.

## Sequence
1. Short problem/value introduction
2. Useful follow-up with specific relevance
3. Proof/process explanation
4. Final close-the-loop message

## Copy Rules
Personalize based on legitimate business context, avoid fake familiarity, keep one CTA and provide an easy opt-out path.

## Target KPIs
Positive reply rate, meeting-booked rate, bounce rate and qualified opportunity rate.

## Deliverables
Prospect criteria, four-touch sequence, subject-line ideas and tracking plan.
