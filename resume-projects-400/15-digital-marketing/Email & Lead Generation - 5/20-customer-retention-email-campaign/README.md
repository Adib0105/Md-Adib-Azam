# Customer Retention Email Campaign

## Objective
Design a lifecycle email strategy that encourages repeat engagement, repeat purchase and stronger customer relationships.

## Lifecycle Messages
Post-purchase thank-you, product/service education, replenishment/reminder, cross-sell where relevant, feedback request and re-engagement.

## Segments
New customers, repeat customers, high-value customers and inactive customers.

## Target KPIs
Repeat purchase rate, retention rate, reactivation rate, click rate and customer lifetime value trend.

## Deliverables
Lifecycle map, segment rules, message themes, CTA framework and retention measurement plan.
