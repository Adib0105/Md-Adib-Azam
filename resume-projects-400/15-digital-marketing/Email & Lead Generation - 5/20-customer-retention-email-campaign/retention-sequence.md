# Customer Retention Email Sequence

## Day 7 — Check-in
Ask whether the customer achieved the expected first outcome and provide help resources.

## Day 21 — Education
Send a useful tip related to the product/service already purchased.

## Day 45 — Feedback
Request honest feedback with a short survey. Do not condition rewards on positive reviews.

## Day 75 — Relevant Cross-sell
Recommend a genuinely complementary product/service based on previous purchase category.

## Day 120 — Win-back
Share what is new and invite the customer back with a real, transparent offer if available.
