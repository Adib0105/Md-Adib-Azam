# Welcome Email Automation Sequence

## Objective
Design a five-email onboarding sequence that welcomes new subscribers, sets expectations and moves them toward a useful next action.

## Sequence
1. Welcome + value promise
2. Best resources / quick win
3. Brand story and trust
4. Core offer / service introduction
5. CTA + preference reminder

## Principles
One primary CTA per email, mobile-friendly copy, clear subject lines and progressive trust building.

## Target KPIs
Open rate, click rate, reply rate, unsubscribe rate and conversion rate.

## Deliverables
Sequence map, subject-line ideas, CTA plan and measurement framework.
