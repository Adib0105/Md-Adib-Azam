# Welcome Email Automation Sequence

Portfolio sample sequence — not tied to a live mailing list.

## Email 1 — Welcome / Day 0
**Subject:** Welcome — here’s what to expect

Thanks for joining. Over the next few emails, you’ll receive practical marketing ideas focused on clearer campaigns, better measurement and realistic next steps.

**CTA:** Explore the starter guide.

## Email 2 — Value / Day 2
**Subject:** Start with one measurable goal

Before choosing a channel, define the action you want: enquiry, purchase, booking, signup or qualified visit. That decision should shape the campaign.

**CTA:** Choose your primary KPI.

## Email 3 — Proof of Process / Day 5
**Subject:** A simple campaign framework

Audience → Offer → Creative → Landing page → Conversion event → Follow-up.

**CTA:** Review the framework.

## Email 4 — Conversion / Day 8
**Subject:** Ready to turn the plan into action?

Use the checklist to identify the next channel or campaign improvement.

**CTA:** Request a campaign review.
