# Restaurant Instagram Growth Campaign

## Objective
Build a 30-day organic Instagram growth plan for a local restaurant focused on reach, saves, profile visits and table enquiries.

## Target Audience
18–40 year-old local food lovers, students, working professionals and families within the restaurant's service area.

## Strategy
- 4 feed posts + 3 reels per week
- Food photography, behind-the-scenes, chef stories and customer proof
- Local hashtags, geo-tags and comment engagement
- Weekend offer and reservation CTA

## Content Pillars
1. Signature dishes
2. Kitchen/BTS
3. Customer reviews
4. Offers
5. Local community content

## Target KPIs
Reach, engagement rate, saves, profile visits, DM enquiries and reservation clicks.

## Deliverables
30-day content calendar, caption framework, hashtag sets, reel ideas and KPI tracking plan.
