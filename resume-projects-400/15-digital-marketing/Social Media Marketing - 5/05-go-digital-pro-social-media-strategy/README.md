# Go Digital Pro — Complete Social Media Strategy

## Objective
Build a full social media positioning and lead-generation strategy for a digital marketing agency.

## Positioning
Practical growth partner for small businesses needing social media, SEO, paid ads, content and digital presence support.

## Content Pillars
- Marketing education
- Client problems and solutions
- Service explainers
- Case-study style breakdowns
- Agency BTS / personal brand

## Channel Plan
Instagram for visual education and leads, LinkedIn for authority and B2B outreach, Facebook for local business reach.

## Funnel
Educational Content → Trust → Profile/Website → Free Audit/Consultation CTA → Qualified Lead.

## Target KPIs
Qualified enquiries, saves, shares, profile visits, consultation bookings and lead conversion rate.

## Deliverables
90-day direction, content pillars, CTA system, posting rhythm and KPI framework.
