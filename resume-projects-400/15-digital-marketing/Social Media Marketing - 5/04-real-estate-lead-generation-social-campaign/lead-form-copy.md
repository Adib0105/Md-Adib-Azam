# Real Estate Lead Form Copy

## Headline
Book a property visit at a time that works for you.

## Intro
Share a few details and receive the property summary, current availability and visit options.

## Fields
- Full name
- Mobile number
- Preferred location
- Budget range
- Property type
- Purchase timeline
- Preferred visit day

## Confirmation
Thanks — your request has been recorded. A representative should confirm availability before any visit is treated as booked.
