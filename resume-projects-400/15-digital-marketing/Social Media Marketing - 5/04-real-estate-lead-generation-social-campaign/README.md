# Real Estate Lead Generation Social Campaign

## Objective
Create a social media lead-generation framework for residential property enquiries without relying only on generic listing posts.

## Audience
First-time buyers, investors, families and working professionals based on location, budget and purchase intent.

## Campaign Structure
1. Property walkthrough reels
2. Price/location comparison posts
3. Amenity highlights
4. Buyer education carousels
5. Lead form / WhatsApp CTA

## Lead Qualification
Budget, preferred location, property type, purchase timeline and financing status.

## Target KPIs
Qualified leads, lead-to-call rate, site-visit bookings, cost per lead target and enquiry response time.

## Deliverables
Content strategy, lead funnel, qualification questions, CTA copy and reporting scorecard.
