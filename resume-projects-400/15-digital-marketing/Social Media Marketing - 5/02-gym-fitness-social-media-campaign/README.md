# Gym & Fitness Social Media Campaign

## Objective
Create a lead-focused social campaign for a local gym to promote memberships, trial sessions and trainer consultations.

## Audience
18–35 year-old beginners, students, professionals and fitness-focused users in the local area.

## Strategy
- Transformation stories and member testimonials
- Trainer tips and short workout reels
- Limited-time trial membership CTA
- Story polls, Q&A and progress challenges

## Funnel
Awareness Reel → Profile Visit → Trial Offer → WhatsApp/DM Lead → Membership Follow-up.

## Target KPIs
Video views, engagement rate, leads, cost per lead target, trial bookings and membership conversions.

## Deliverables
Campaign concept, 30-day posting plan, CTA library, lead funnel and reporting framework.
