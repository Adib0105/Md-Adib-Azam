# Gym Campaign Creative Brief

## Audience
Beginners aged 18–35 who want a structured, approachable fitness routine.

## Message
Progress comes from consistency, coaching and a plan — not extreme promises.

## Content Pillars
1. Trainer education
2. Member stories
3. Exercise technique
4. Facility proof
5. Trial-membership offers

## CTA Ladder
Save → Follow → DM `START` → Book trial → Join.

## Guardrail
Avoid guaranteed weight-loss claims or unrealistic before/after promises.
