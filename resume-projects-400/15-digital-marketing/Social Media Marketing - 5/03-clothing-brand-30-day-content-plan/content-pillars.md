# Clothing Brand Content Pillars

- **Product:** drops, details, styling, restocks
- **Education:** sizing, care, outfit combinations
- **Brand:** mood, values, behind-the-scenes
- **Community:** UGC, polls, customer features
- **Trust:** reviews, shipping, returns, FAQs

Recommended mix: 30% product, 25% education, 20% community, 15% brand, 10% direct conversion.
