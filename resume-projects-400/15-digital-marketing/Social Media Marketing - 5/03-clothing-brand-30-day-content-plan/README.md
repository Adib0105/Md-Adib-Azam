# Clothing Brand — 30-Day Content Plan

## Objective
Design a complete monthly content system for a fashion/streetwear brand that balances brand building, product discovery and sales.

## Content Mix
- Product showcase: 30%
- Lifestyle/UGC: 25%
- Reels/trends: 20%
- Education/style tips: 15%
- Offers/community: 10%

## Weekly Rhythm
Monday product focus, Tuesday styling tip, Wednesday reel, Thursday customer/UGC, Friday offer, weekend lifestyle/community.

## Conversion Path
Content → Product interest → Profile/link click → Product page → Purchase/DM.

## Target KPIs
Reach, saves, shares, link clicks, add-to-cart rate and sales attribution.

## Deliverables
30-day calendar, content pillars, caption templates, CTA map and measurement plan.
