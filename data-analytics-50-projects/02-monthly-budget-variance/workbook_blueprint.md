# Workbook Blueprint

- ROAS: `=[@revenue]/[@spend]`

- Conditional-format ROAS below 2.0 in red.
- Build channel comparison chart and KPI cards.

2. `Calculations` — helper columns and checks
3. `Dashboard` — KPI cards, charts, filters and a one-sentence recommendation
